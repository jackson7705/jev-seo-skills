# How Much Does a Roof Replacement Cost in Houston?

A shingle roof replacement in Houston typically costs $9,000 to $18,000 for a 2,000 sq ft home. Most homeowners land near the middle of that range.

## What changes the price

Four things move the number most: the roofing material, the size of the roof, how steep the pitch is, and how many old layers have to be torn off. Rotten decking found during tear-off adds cost too.

## Metal vs shingles

Metal roofing costs roughly twice as much as asphalt shingles up front, but it can last 40 to 70 years, so the cost per year is often lower.
